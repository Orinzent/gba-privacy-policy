Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 22, 2026 | App: GBA Emulator | Developer: mecorven
Summary: GBA Emulator is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data. All game save files, configurations, and settings remain 100% local on your device.

1. Information Collection and Use

GBA Emulator is a fully offline application. We do not collect any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers.

2. App Permissions

To provide core emulation features, the application requests the following device permissions:

  • Vibration Control (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used solely to provide haptic feedback when pressing virtual controller buttons on screen.
  • Storage / File Access: Used exclusively to allow you to select and load your legal GBA ROM files, write battery save files (SRAM/Flash), save state snapshots, and store controller configuration files directly on your local device storage.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

We do not integrate any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, tracking tools, or external services. No network traffic is generated by this application.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Since no personal data is ever collected by the app, parents can be assured that their children's privacy is fully protected.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

All application data—including game save states, settings, and custom configurations—is stored exclusively on your device. You can delete all application data at any time by clearing the application storage via your device settings or by uninstalling the application.
